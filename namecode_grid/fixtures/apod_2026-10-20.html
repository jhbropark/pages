An Anticrepuscular Rainbow Fan

Explanation: NASA's October 20, 2026 article describes an unusual, incomplete rainbow appearing in a sunlit fan. The apparent location of the Sun is misleading; ordinary rainbows form opposite the Sun. Gaps in clouds let rays cross the sky and converge in front, creating anticrepuscular rays. The observation was made near sunset in Presenzano, Italy.
